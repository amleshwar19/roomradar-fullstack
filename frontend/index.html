✨ AI Room Finder — just describe what you need

Type naturally, e.g. “I need an AC room on the ground floor for me and my team for the next 2 hours.” The AI parses your needs, checks every timetable, and returns rooms free for the whole duration.

About the data. Room occupancy is derived from the 10 official odd-semester timetables (13 sections). Each section's printed Venue is its home classroom; a room counts as occupied while its section has a class, and free during gaps, lunch, and periods when the class is timetabled elsewhere (labs, workshops, CDC/NSS/Yoga). 4 first-year sections print no venue, so 8 physical rooms are tracked: IST 225, 227, 211 (floor 2) · IST 411, 416 (floor 4) · IST 518, 519 (floor 5) · IST 602 (floor 6). Amenities shown (AC, projector, capacity) are standard IST-block classroom defaults. Weekends & outside 9:00–16:50 → all rooms free.
